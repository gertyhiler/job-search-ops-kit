# Session Type: Monthly Strategy Review

Primary role: `strategist`. Runs monthly (default 1st at 10:00). Same contract as weekly consolidation but with a wider window and higher-impact threshold.

Flow matches `weekly-consolidation.md`, with:
- 30-day funnel slice.
- Focus on structural shifts (ICP, channel mix), not micro-tweaks.
- Allowed to submit up to 2 proposals if clearly orthogonal.
